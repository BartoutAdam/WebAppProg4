Adam presents plays by Ibsen

The Lady From The Sea

A short time later we resume

Wangel

I am glad to see you, my dear girls.

[He looks towards the house and smiles.]

Hilde

We are glad that you are back, father.

Bolette

Have you heard anything from Arnholm?

[She looks towards the garden gate.]

Wangel

He should be here very soon.

Hilde

Then perhaps we should wait for him.

Bolette

Yes, let us wait here.
